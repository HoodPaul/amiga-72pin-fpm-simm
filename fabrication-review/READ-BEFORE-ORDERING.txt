HY8M A7 DRAFT GERBERS: TECHNICAL REVIEW ONLY

These files are exported from the four-layer user-edited A7 PCB with the added
JMC logo. They are not an approved fabrication release.

Nominal outline: 107.95 x 25.4 mm. Four copper layers. Proposed finished
thickness: 1.2 mm; dielectric thicknesses/copper weights require confirmation.
Intended finish: black solder mask, white silkscreen and wear-resistant
hard-gold edge contacts. Decorative gold/ENIG and selective finger plating are
separate process choices. Gerbers do not encode colour or plating grade.

Layer allocation:
F.Cu: front signals/pads
In1.Cu: GND plane
In2.Cu: signal tracks with +5 V fill
B.Cu: rear signals/pads

Nominal minimum tracks/spacing: 0.20/0.20 mm.
Through vias: 0.60 mm copper diameter, 0.30 mm drill.
Routed supply tracks: 0.40 mm, with 0.25 mm connector joins and supply fill.

The inherited 144 contact-edge findings concern a 0.2286 mm gold-finger pad
setback against the generic 0.50 mm rule. Review finger length, plating/mask
openings, edge setback, finished thickness and bevel profile with the fabricator.
No arbitrary 45-degree bevel is specified. Refer to the current DRC/validation
reports for complete findings and project check settings; this note does not
claim a full clean DRC, schematic-parity or ERC result.

The two retaining holes are closed circular Edge.Cuts profiles from the
mechanical template, not duplicate NPTH drill hits. Review their outline/routing
interpretation and dimensions/tolerances. The key notch and contact positions
are unchanged from the accepted reference geometry.

Four copper layers, front/rear mask, silkscreen, paste and outline files are
included. The common paste files include unfitted component positions.
Assembly and stencil use must follow the selected variant BOM. A true 4 MB
single-sided build omits rear ICs/capacitors and R1-R4. The 8 MB build uses
R1/R2 = 0 ohm and R3/R4 = 10 kohm each. PD strap references are PD1-PD4 in the
PCB, schematics and BOMs; fitted straps are 0 ohm and absent straps are open.

A7 preserves the uploaded custom silkscreen, including the front-left UK
Computing artwork. JMC is added at the front top right as filled white artwork,
18.000 x 3.480 mm, ink bounds x193..211, y87.06..90.54 mm. The user's rear
assembly instructions are under U4 and the PD table under U3, covered when rear
ICs are fitted. Original glyphs and positioning are retained. Review fine logo
details with the legend process; appearance previews show illustrative colours.

Exact native comparison confirms the same 864 tracks, 195 vias, 336 pads,
17 component placements, 13 Edge.Cuts primitives, 2 zone outlines and 10 filled
copper polygons as the uploaded PCB and retained A6 reference. Historical
A3-A6 geometry also agrees. Reports are in ../docs/copper-comparison.txt/json.

No physical donor, assembled board, timing, refresh or Amiga hardware validation
has been completed. The intended 8 MB module providing 2 MB Chip RAM remains an
inference from host wiring. Read ../README.md and the current validation and
compatibility reports before fabrication.
