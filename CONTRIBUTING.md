# Contributing

This project is maintained by Paul of UK Computing with Jon of JMC Electronics
and AI assistance. Read [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) for attribution, artwork retention
and the current licensing position; no project-wide standard licence is selected.

1. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), [current handover](docs/AI-HANDOVER.md) and relevant technical records.
2. Create a focused branch and independent working copy; preserve unrelated/manual edits.
3. Describe the actual problem and retain the delivered CAD as the baseline. Do not make speculative electrical substitutions or reroute working geometry as incidental cleanup.
4. Validate at the appropriate level using [TESTING.md](docs/TESTING.md). A documentation task needs a scope/link/content check; a circuit change needs fresh relevant exports and design review.
5. Update the handover and authoritative guide; record important reasoning in `docs/decisions/`.
6. Open a draft PR explaining what changed, evidence, remaining findings and manufacturing/hardware impact. Obtain task-specific merge or fabrication-order authorisation.

Do not silently remove the documented DRC/parity findings or convert inferred
compatibility into tested claims. Retain reference files and historical reports.
Use British English and synthetic/non-sensitive identifiers in public evidence.
Never post shipping addresses, private order links, credentials or customer data.
